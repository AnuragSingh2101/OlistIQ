# OlistIQ: Commercial & Operational Performance Report

**Dataset Coverage:** 2016 – 2018 (Brazilian E-Commerce / Olist)  
**Total Analyzed Transactions:** 99,441 orders | R$ 16.0M Gross Merchandise Value  

---

## Executive Summary

This report outlines the primary commercial dynamics, revenue drivers, and delivery bottlenecks identified across the Olist marketplace ecosystem. The objective is to evaluate merchant and logistics performance and recommend operational enhancements to improve customer satisfaction and order retention.

---

## 1. Commercial Performance & Revenue Drivers

- **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV):** R$ 16.0M generated across ~99.4k transactions.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** R$ 160.85 per transaction.
- **Peak Seasonality:** Peak commercial demand occurred during **November 2017 (Black Friday)**, generating over R$ 1.19M in a single month (a ~70% MoM surge in order volume).
- **Dominant Categories:**
  1. *Health & Beauty* (~R$ 1.25M GMV)
  2. *Watches & Gifts* (~R$ 1.20M GMV)
  3. *Bed, Bath & Table* (~R$ 1.03M GMV)
  4. *Sports & Leisure* (~R$ 0.98M GMV)
  5. *Computers & Accessories* (~R$ 0.91M GMV)

---

## 2. Logistics & Delivery SLA Analysis

- **National Average Delivery Time:** 12.5 calendar days from order purchase to customer delivery.
- **On-Time Fulfillment Rate:** 92.1% of orders were delivered within the estimated SLA window.
- **Regional Disparities:**
  - **Fastest Fulfillment:** São Paulo (`SP`), Paraná (`PR`), and Minas Gerais (`MG`) averaged **8 to 11 days**.
  - **Logistics Bottlenecks:** Northern and North-Eastern states (e.g. `RR`, `AP`, `AM`, `AL`) experienced transit times exceeding **25 to 30 days**, with SLA delay rates climbing above **15%**.

---

## 3. Payment Economics

- **Credit Card** dominates transaction volume, accounting for **75.8% of total revenue** and offering the highest average basket size (AOV: R$ 163.30).
- **Boleto Bancário** represents **19.1% of GMV**, serving as a critical payment rail for unbanked consumers.
- **Vouchers & Debit Cards** comprise the remaining ~5.1%.

---

## 4. Key Recommendations

1. **Regional Fulfillment Hubs:** Establish decentralized fulfillment centers in the North-East to reduce shipping times from 26 days to under 14 days.
2. **Dynamic Delivery Estimates:** Adjust estimated delivery dates dynamically for distant postal codes to prevent avoidable SLA breach penalties.
3. **Targeted Category Promotions:** Bundle top-performing categories (*Bed Bath & Table* with *Housewares*) to increase Average Order Value.
